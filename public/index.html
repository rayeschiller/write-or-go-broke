A few things
you might ask…

What if what I write sucks?+

Perfect. Write it anyway. You're committing to 10 minutes of standup writing, not 10 minutes of genius.

How do check-ins work?+

During the challenge, you'll confirm that you wrote for at least 10 minutes that day. A screenshot or photo is optional. We start on the honor system. Check-ins open November 18.

Where does my money actually go?+

Your $150 covers all 30 days. A successful check-in protects $5. Each missed day's $5 goes into that day's pool, split equally among the people who checked in. There is no platform fee. If everyone checks in, everyone keeps their own $5.

When does the next cohort start?+

The next cohort starts November 18. Sign up and Venmo $150 to @Raye-Schiller to secure your spot. After your payment and enrollment are confirmed, use your private setup link to create your login. Check in by midnight Pacific each day.

What if nobody checks in?+

The zero-check-in rule will be finalized and shared before payment opens, along with how payout rounding and withdrawals work. Joining this list does not commit or charge you.
